# Plan 04-01: Campaign Tactical Systems

## Goal

Deliver the locked Phase 4 specification end-to-end in the LibGDX campaign client and synchronized source maps.

**Verify:** Visibility math tests, full core suite, GWT distribution, Spring tests and Docker multi-stage build.
